Privacy

Short version: Filewinch doesn't collect anything about you or your files.

The app

  • No telemetry. Filewinch sends no analytics, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • Your connections are yours. Filewinch talks directly to the servers and services you connect to. Nothing passes through us.
  • Cloud sign-in goes straight to each provider. Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and Box sign in through your web browser on the provider's own page. Filewinch receives a token from the provider and never sees your password.
  • Secrets stay on your computer. Passwords, key passphrases and sign-in tokens are kept in your system's secret store: Keychain on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows, Secret Service on Linux. Your saved sites and preferences are ordinary files in your user folder.
  • Licence checks go to Polar. If you enter a licence key, Filewinch sends it to Polar, our payment provider, to confirm it's valid. Nothing about your files, servers or usage is included.

Buying a licence

Checkout is run by Polar, who process the payment, sales tax and your receipt under their own privacy policy. We receive what we need to honour the purchase, such as your email address and order details.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. The download page asks GitHub's public API for the latest release, straight from your browser. GitHub may log requests to its servers as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
