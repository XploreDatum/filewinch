Check your download

Every release lists SHA-256 checksums for its files: checksums on the release page. Compare with shasum -a 256 <file> on macOS, sha256sum <file> on Linux, or Get-FileHash <file> in PowerShell.

Installing on Linux

Make the AppImage executable (chmod +x Filewinch-*.AppImage) and run it. Or unpack the tarball and run ./install.sh, which installs to ~/.local; ./install.sh --uninstall removes it.
