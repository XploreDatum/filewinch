Thank you.

Your licence keeps Filewinch independent and moving. Here's what happens next.

Your key

Polar has emailed your licence key to the address you used at checkout. It usually arrives within a few minutes. If it hasn't, check your spam folder, or sign in to the Polar customer portal with the same email address: your key and receipt are always there.

Enter it in Filewinch

  1. Open Filewinch. If you haven't installed it yet, download it here.
  2. In Filewinch, click Enter Licence Key… in the banner at the bottom of the window. (Or open Settings, Preferences on Linux, with ⌘, on a Mac or Ctrl+, on Windows and Linux, and use the Licence section.)
  3. Paste your key and confirm. Filewinch checks it with Polar, and the banner disappears.

Need a hand?

For anything about your purchase, use the Polar customer portal. For problems with the app itself, open an issue on GitHub.
