Questions

What does the licence actually change?

Only the banner. Free and licensed copies are the same app with the same features. Entering a key hides the banner and tells us you're behind the project.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once. There's nothing to renew.

How do I get my key?

Polar emails it to you right after checkout. It's also kept in your Polar customer portal, so you can find it again at any time. How to enter it.

Why is there tax on top?

US$14 is the price before tax. Polar acts as the seller of record and adds the sales tax or VAT your country requires at checkout.

Does a licence check send my data anywhere?

Checking a key talks to Polar and sends the key, nothing about your files or servers. Filewinch has no telemetry. See the privacy page.

Filewinch is open source. Why pay?

You don't have to. The code is under the GNU AGPL and you can build it yourself. Paying is how the project stays maintained.

Something went wrong with my order.

Your receipt and key are in the Polar customer portal, which is also where you can get in touch about an order.
